For a long time I kept notes in a dozen different apps and never looked at them again. Writing in public seemed like a way to make the notes actually *useful*, even if I'm the only reader.

## What I want this blog to be

A few things I'm aiming for:

1. **Short posts are fine.** Not everything needs to be an essay.
2. **Show the work.** Screenshots, code, half-finished ideas.
3. **Be honest about what didn't work.**

> The best way to learn something is to try to explain it to someone else.

That quote gets attributed to a lot of different people. Whoever said it first was right.

## What I'll write about

- Projects I'm building
- Things I'm learning in class
- Trips and photos
- Anything else that seems worth remembering
